# Study Plan (3–15 Days)

**Summary**: Day-by-day practice schedule to reach 79+ in 3–15 days, structured by skill.

**Sources**: `3–15 Days PTE Study Plan _ Full Strategy to Score 79+🔥.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-19

---

## Phase 1: Speaking (Days 1–3)

Each practice session has three blocks:

### Block 1 — Fluency Only (15–20 min)
- Open Read Aloud or Describe Image questions.
- **Do not give preparation time.** Start speaking the moment you see the question.
- Focus entirely on fluency score — ignore content score completely.
- Target: fluency 4.5 or 5/5 in practice portal.
- This trains your brain to default to a flowing, rhythmic speech pattern.

See [[fluency-training]] for the full method.

### Block 2 — Content Only (20–30 min)
- 5–6 Describe Image questions
- 2–3 Retell Lecture questions
- 1–2 Summarize Group Discussion questions
- 1–2 Respond to Situation questions
- Focus only on content score — target 5+ or 6/6. Ignore fluency.
- Use [[note-taking]] method for Retell Lecture and Summarize Group Discussion.

### Block 3 — Both Together (20–30 min)
- Practice with full focus on both fluency and content simultaneously.
- By day 3, your brain is pre-trained for each. Combining becomes natural.
- Allocate brain: ~60–70% fluency, ~30–40% content.

---

## Phase 2: Writing (Days 4–13)

### Daily Writing Practice
- **1 essay per day** (2 if time allows). Analyze model answers: structure, vocabulary, sentence variety. See [[essay-writing]].
- **20–25 Write from Dictation** questions per day. Use three-step method. See [[write-from-dictation]].
- **1–2 Summarize Spoken Text** questions per day. Use [[note-taking]] method.
- **10 min Skills Pro AI app** — spelling mastery (500 PTE spellings, 2,000 spelling mastery exercises).

---

## Phase 3: Reading (Days 4–15)

### Daily Reading Practice
- **2 full reading mocks** per day (or 10–12 Reading/Writing FITB questions + 10–12 Reading FITB questions + 2–3 Reorder Paragraph).
- **1 hour of reading activity** daily — this is non-negotiable for score improvement. Reading skill must improve for FITB scores to rise.
- **10–15 min Skills Pro AI**: collocation exercises + vocabulary + Rapid Reader (speed reading tool).

Reading improvement is cumulative — scores rise as underlying reading skill improves, not just from learning question tactics. Start this early.

---

## Daily Time Budget

| Block | Time |
|---|---|
| Speaking practice | 60–70 min |
| Writing practice | 45–60 min |
| Reading practice + activity | 90 min |
| Skills Pro AI app | 30 min |
| **Total** | **~4 hours/day** |

For a compressed 3-day speaking focus, reduce to speaking only in the first 3 days, then shift to reading and writing.

---

## Key Principle

> "Fluency is trained first, content is trained second, then they are combined."

Don't try to do both from day one. Split training creates faster neural adaptation.
