## How scores are reported

- One **Overall score** plus four **communicative skills** (Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing). All on a **10–90** scale.
- **The Overall score is NOT the average of the four skill scores.** It is computed from performance across all questions.
- You complete **65–75 questions** across **22 question types** in a test (max length 2h15m).
- **Integrated-skills questions** (e.g. Listening+Speaking, Reading+Writing, Listening+Writing) contribute to **both** skill scores at once. This is why tasks like Retell Lecture, SST, and Write from Dictation are high-value — one task feeds two scores.

## How scoring works: AI + human

- All questions are AI-scored. Human experts originally trained the engines on every Speaking and Writing response, and the system replicates those standards.
- Humans also do live scoring in two cases:
  1. **Anomalies** — responses too quiet/unclear for reliable machine scoring.
  2. **Additional Content review** for specific task types (below).
- **Content = 0 stops scoring.** If Content is 0, no other trait (fluency, pronunciation, etc.) is scored and the question contributes nothing.
- **Memorised/pre-prepared material is classified as an irrelevant response → Content 0.** This is the official basis for every "don't use memorised templates" warning.
- For Content, if AI and human disagree, a **second human** makes the final call.

## Per-task trait maxima (official)

### Part 1 — Speaking & Writing

| Task | Questions | Skills | Traits (max) |
|------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Read Aloud | 6–7 | Speaking | Content (per-prompt) + Pronunciation 5 + Oral Fluency 5 |
| Repeat Sentence | 10–12 | Listening + Speaking | Content 3 + Pronunciation 5 + Oral Fluency 5 |
| Describe Image | 5–6 | Speaking | Content 6 + Pronunciation 5 + Oral Fluency 5 |
| Retell Lecture | 2–3 | Listening + Speaking | Content 6 + Pronunciation 5 + Oral Fluency 5 |
| Answer Short Question | 5–6 | **Listening** | Vocabulary 1/0 (correct or incorrect) |
| Summarize Group Discussion | 2–3 | Listening + Speaking | Content 6 + Pronunciation 5 + Oral Fluency 5 |
| Respond to a Situation | 2–3 | Speaking | Content 6 + Pronunciation 5 + Oral Fluency 5 |
| Summarize Written Text | 2 | Reading + Writing | Content 4 + Form 1 + Grammar 2 + Vocabulary 2 |
| Write Essay | 1 | Writing | Content 6 + Form 2 + Dev/Structure/Coherence 6 + Grammar 2 + General Linguistic Range 6 + Vocabulary Range 2 + Spelling 2 |

> **Repeat Sentence Content is max 3**, not a 5-band scale: 3 = all words in correct sequence, 2 = ≥50%, 1 = <50%, 0 = almost nothing.

> **Answer Short Question scores Listening only** (Vocabulary 1/0), not Speaking. It is correct/incorrect.

### Part 2 — Reading (all "Not applicable" for traits; scored right/wrong or partial)

| Task | Questions | Scoring |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Fill in the Blanks (Dropdown / R&W) | 5–6 | Partial credit per blank |
| Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers | 2–3 | +1 correct, −1 incorrect |
| Reorder Paragraph | 2–3 | Partial credit per correct adjacent pair |
| Fill in the Blanks (Drag & Drop) | 4–5 | Partial credit per blank |
| Multiple Choice, Single Answer | 2–3 | Correct/incorrect |

### Part 3 — Listening

| Task | Questions | Skills | Traits (max) |
|------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Summarize Spoken Text | 1 | Listening + Writing | Content 4 + Form 2 + Grammar 2 + Vocabulary 2 + Spelling 2 |
| Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers | 2–3 | Listening | +1 / −1 |
| Fill in the Blanks (Type In) | 2–3 | Listening | 1 per correct word |
| Highlight Correct Summary | 2–3 | Listening + Reading | Correct/incorrect |
| Multiple Choice, Single Answer | 2–3 | Listening | Correct/incorrect |
| Select Missing Word | 1–2 | Listening | Correct/incorrect |
| Highlight Incorrect Words | 2–3 | Listening + Reading | +1 / −1 per word |
| Write from Dictation | 3–4 | Listening + Writing | 1 per correct word spelled correctly |

## SST and SWT Form: the exact (softer) rules

Coaches teach "outside the word band = 0." The official Form rubrics are more forgiving:

- **SWT:** Form 1 = one complete sentence within 5–75 words; Form 0 = multiple sentences, or <5 / >75 words, or all caps.
- **SST:** Form 2 = 50–70 words; Form **1 = 40–49 or 71–100 words** (partial credit); Form 0 = <40 or >100 words, or caps/no punctuation/bullets.

Still aim for the full-credit band — the partial band is a safety net, not a target.

## IELTS alignment (updated July 2025)

Pearson's current best estimate (source: Score Guide, p. 12). Useful for translating a target:

| IELTS | PTE Overall | PTE Listening | PTE Reading | PTE Speaking | PTE Writing |
|-------|-------------|---------------|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| 6.0 | 47–54 | 47–52 | 48–53 | 54–65 | 51–59 |
| 6.5 | 55–62 | 53–57 | 54–58 | 66–75 | 60–68 |
| 7.0 | 63–70 | 58–63 | 59–64 | 76–82 | 69–76 |
| 7.5 | 71–78 | 64–68 | 65–69 | 83–87 | 77–84 |
| 8.0 | **79–85** | 69–74 | 70–74 | 88–89 | 85–89 |
| 8.5 | 86–89 | 75–80 | 75–80 | 90 | 90 |

**A 79+ overall (the wiki's target) maps to roughly IELTS 8.0.** Note the skills are not uniform: Speaking saturates fast (PTE 88 ≈ IELTS 8.0) while Listening/Reading need ~69–74 for the same band.

## CEFR alignment

PTE 76–84 ≈ **C1**; 85–90 reaches into **C2**; 59–75 ≈ B2 (source: Score Guide, p. 11). A 79+ target is solidly C1.

## Admission benchmarks (typical)

- Foundation courses: 36–50
- Undergraduate: 51–60
- Postgraduate: 57–67

(Always confirm against the specific institution.)

## Automated scoring engines

- **Writing:** Intelligent Essay Assessor (IEA), powered by the KAT engine using Latent Semantic Analysis — it evaluates *meaning*, not surface keywords. This is why semantically empty, keyword-stuffed responses score poorly.
- **Speaking:** Versant technology — recognises words and evaluates segments, syllables, and phrases statistically.
