# PTE Overview

**Summary**: The PTE Academic is a computer-based English proficiency test with 3 sections and 22 question types, primarily AI-scored with human Content review on seven task types.

**Sources**: `Crack PTE Exam in 1 Hour _ Full PTE Course 2026 _ 22 Modules Live Demo.md`, `PTE Academic & UKVI test format_ Speaking & Writing.md`, `PTE Academic & UKVI test format_ Reading.md`, `PTE Academic & UKVI test format_ Listening.md`, `PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf` (Pearson, official)

**Last updated**: 2026-06-04

## Test Structure

| Section | Duration | Question Types | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part 1: Speaking & Writing | 76–84 min | 9 | Speaking, Writing |
| Part 2: Reading | 23–30 min | 5 | Reading |
| Part 3: Listening | 31–39 min | 8 | Listening, Writing |

Total: 22 question types; 65–75 questions in any given test. The exam is **primarily AI-scored**, but seven task types also receive a **human expert review of Content** before the score is finalised (Describe Image, Retell Lecture, Respond to a Situation, Summarize Group Discussion, Summarize Written Text, Write Essay, Summarize Spoken Text). Memorised/pre-prepared material is treated as irrelevant → Content 0. See [[pte-score-guide]].

> Reading duration: the official guide states ~23–30 min; coaching sources ([[reading-modules]], [[reading-fill-in-the-blanks]]) report an experienced timer of ~20–25 min. Treat the section as tight either way.

## Target Score

- 79+ is the typical migration/visa threshold.
- 90/90 is perfect across all skills.

## PTE Academic vs PTE Core

Key differences called out in the sources:

- **Read Aloud**: Academic = 5-8 marks (speaking only, treated as near-dummy). Core = 30+ marks (speaking + reading).
- **Retell Lecture**: Academic only — not present in Core.
- **Essay Writing**: Academic only. Core uses **Email Writing** instead.
- **Summarize Written Text**: Academic = one sentence, 5–75 words. Core = multiple sentences, 25–50 words.
- **Respond to a Situation**: **Added to PTE Academic in August 2025** (previously Core-only). Now present in both. See [[respond-to-a-situation]].

## AI Evaluation Logic

The AI rewards:
- **Fluency** over perfection — no fumbles, no hesitation, rhythmic speech.
- **Content proximity** — words from the audio or image that appear in your answer. Note: the writing engine (KAT/LSA) evaluates *meaning*, not just keyword overlap, so semantically empty keyword lists score poorly. See [[pte-score-guide]].
- **Spelling precision** — one spelling mistake = one mark lost in essay.
- **No large pitch variation** — male candidates should stay above 140 Hz pitch, female above 200 Hz. *(Coaching guidance from the practice-portal indicator, not in the official Score Guide — the official pronunciation rubric scores clarity/intelligibility and states accent does not matter. Treat the Hz thresholds as practice-portal heuristics, not exam rules.)*
