# Scoring Strategy

**Summary**: Which modules give the most marks, which to skip, and how to allocate effort for a 79+ score.

**Sources**: `Crack PTE Exam in 1 Hour _ Full PTE Course 2026 _ 22 Modules Live Demo.md`, `3–15 Days PTE Study Plan.md`, `PTE-Academic-Test-Taker-Score-Guide.pdf` (Pearson, official)

**Last updated**: 2026-06-04

> The marks below are **coaching estimates** of each module's contribution, not official figures. For official per-trait maxima, question counts, and skill mappings, see [[pte-score-guide]]. Where the two differ, the official guide is authoritative.

## Target translation (official IELTS alignment, July 2025)

The wiki's **79+ overall** target ≈ **IELTS 8.0**. Skills don't move uniformly — Speaking saturates early, Listening/Reading lag:

| To reach IELTS 8.0 | PTE needed |
|---|---|
| Overall | 79–85 |
| Speaking | 88–89 (saturates fastest) |
| Writing | 85–89 |
| Listening | 69–74 |
| Reading | 70–74 |

A 79+ overall sits in **CEFR C1**. Full table: [[pte-score-guide]].

## Skip List (Low Marks per the coaching source)

These modules have low individual weight per the source instructor. Click an answer and move on:

- **Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers** (Reading) — note: **−1 per wrong option**, so don't select extras
- **Multiple Choice, Single Answer** (Reading)
- **Multiple Choice, Multiple Answer** (Listening) — also **−1 per wrong option**
- **Highlight Correct Summary** (Listening)
- **Multiple Choice, Single Answer** (Listening)
- **Select Missing Word** (Listening)

Skipping these frees up time for Write from Dictation and Reading FITB — which together account for 60+ marks.

> **Correction (2026-06-04):** **Answer Short Question** was previously listed here as a "Speaking, ~0" skip. The official guide shows it scores **Listening** (Vocabulary correct/incorrect), not Speaking — a correct one-word answer is a free Listening point. Don't blind-skip it; answer when you know it. See [[speaking-modules]] and [[pte-score-guide]].
>
> Also note the negative marking on Multiple Choice Multiple Answers (both sections): a wrong tick subtracts a point, so if you do attempt them, only select options you're confident about.

## Top 5 Modules to Master

1. **Write from Dictation** — highest single-module mark in the exam (~55–60 combined writing + listening). See [[write-from-dictation]].
2. **Repeat Sentence** — ~44–45 marks combined. See [[speaking-modules]].
3. **Reading FITB (both types)** — ~50+ marks combined. See [[reading-fill-in-the-blanks]].
4. **Write Essay** — 26–30 marks writing. See [[essay-writing]].
5. **Summarize Group Discussion** — ~30–36 marks combined. See [[summarize-group-discussion]].

## Score Lever: Spelling

Spelling affects multiple modules simultaneously:
- Essay Writing: 2 marks (lose 1 per error, 0 after 2 errors)
- Summarize Spoken Text: 2 marks
- Write from Dictation: every word is a mark
- Listening FITB: every blank is a mark

Improving spelling has the broadest cross-module impact of any single skill.

---

## Score Lever: Fluency

Fluency affects every speaking module. Even when content is wrong, fluency keeps marks high. Train fluency before content. See [[fluency-training]].
