# Reading Modules

## Timer Warning

The Reading section timer was reduced from ~33–35 minutes to 20–25 minutes in recent updates. Old brute-force reading strategies no longer work. You must use a grammar + collocation approach to solve Fill in the Blanks accurately and quickly.

## 1. Fill in the Blanks (Dropdown) — Reading & Writing FITB

- **Prompt**: Text up to 300 words with dropdown gaps
- **Questions**: 5–6 per exam
- **Marks (Academic)**: ~25–30 marks reading
- **Marks (Core)**: ~25–30 marks reading + ~25–30 marks writing

**Strategy**: This is a top-priority module. Use grammar rules (part of speech) and collocation knowledge to pick the right word. Read before and after each blank. Skim overall meaning first. Don't spend more than 3 minutes per question.

See [[reading-fill-in-the-blanks]] for the full grammar + collocation method.

---

## 2. Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers

- **Prompt**: Text up to 350 words, select all correct answers
- **Questions**: 1–2 per exam
- **Marks**: Low — skip-worthy

**Strategy**: The moment you see this question, click one answer and move on. Do not spend time analyzing. Every minute here is stolen from FITB questions worth 25+ marks. Negative marking applies (wrong selections deduct marks), but one random click and moving on minimizes damage.

---

## 3. Reorder Paragraph

- **Prompt**: Text boxes up to 150 words, drag to correct order
- **Questions**: 2–3 per exam
- **Marks (Academic)**: ~5–6 marks
- **Marks (Core)**: ~5–6 marks

**Strategy**: Not very important. Find the topic sentence (no "but" or "it" at start, introduces the subject). Then follow logical flow — each sentence continues the idea of the previous one. For Academic: max 1 minute per question, then randomly choose and move on. For Core: up to 1.5–2 minutes.

Method: Find the opening sentence → trace pronoun/connector chains to link the rest.

---

## 4. Fill in the Blanks (Drag and Drop) — Reading FITB

- **Prompt**: Text up to 80 words, drag words from a box
- **Questions**: 4–5 per exam
- **Marks**: ~25+ marks reading

**Strategy**: Same grammar + collocation approach as the dropdown FITB. More words are provided than gaps — eliminate wrong options using grammar (e.g., after "will" use base verb, after "the" use noun). Use collocations to confirm. Do easy blanks first, return to difficult ones.

See [[reading-fill-in-the-blanks]] for full technique.

---

## 5. Multiple Choice, Single Answer

- **Prompt**: Text up to 300 words, one correct answer
- **Questions**: 1–2 per exam
- **Marks**: Low — skip-worthy

**Strategy**: Same as Multiple Choice Multiple Answers — click one and move on. Do not invest time here. The marks do not justify the time cost relative to FITB modules.

---

## Priority Order for Reading

1. **Reading/Writing FITB (dropdown)** — 25–30 marks, do first
2. **Reading FITB (drag and drop)** — 25+ marks, do second
3. **Reorder Paragraph** — 5–6 marks, max 1 min each
4. **Multiple Choice (any)** — click one and skip
