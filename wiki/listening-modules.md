## Critical Insight

Listening marks also come from **speaking modules**. Repeat Sentence (~30 marks), Retell Lecture (~10–12 marks), and Summarize Group Discussion (~15–18 marks) all feed listening. See [[speaking-modules]] and [[scoring-strategy]].

The dedicated Listening section contributes marks primarily through: Summarize Spoken Text, Listening FITB, Highlight Incorrect Words, and Write from Dictation.

---

## 1. Summarize Spoken Text

- **Audio**: 60–90 seconds
- **Time**: 10 minutes total (including audio)
- **Response**: 50–70 words (Academic) / 20–30 words (Core)
- **Skills**: Listening and Writing
- **Questions**: 1 (Academic) / 1–2 (Core)
- **Marks (Academic)**: ~10–12 marks listening, ~12–15 marks writing

**Strategy**: Take notes while listening — same alternating method as [[note-taking]]. After audio ends, transform notes into grammatically correct sentences. Content is 4 marks (Academic) — get 4–5 exact sentences from the audio. Spelling and grammar must be correct (unlike speaking). Target 58–65 words. Use template only to pad word count if below 50: "The lecture is about... Firstly, the speaker described... In conclusion..."

---

## 2. Multiple Choice, Multiple Answer

- **Audio**: 80–120 seconds
- **Skills**: Listening
- **Questions**: 1–2 per exam
- **Marks**: ~0 — skip-worthy

**Strategy**: Let audio play (next button only activates after audio ends). The moment audio ends, select one answer and click next. Do not analyze options.

---

## 3. Fill in the Blanks (Type In)

- **Audio**: 30–60 seconds
- **Skills**: Listening
- **Questions**: 2–3 per exam
- **Marks (Academic)**: ~8–10 marks listening
- **Marks (Core)**: ~12–15 marks listening + writing

**Strategy**: Skim the transcript before audio starts to understand the topic and spot where blanks are. Use the erasable noteboard to jot blanks as you hear them. After audio, type in. Spelling matters — one wrong spelling = one mark lost.

---

## 4. Highlight Correct Summary

- **Audio**: 30–90 seconds
- **Skills**: Listening and Reading
- **Questions**: 1–2 per exam
- **Marks**: ~0 — skip-worthy

**Strategy**: Let audio play. When next button activates, select one and click next. Do not read the summaries while listening — it's impossible to do both. No marks have been confirmed from this module.

---

## 5. Multiple Choice, Single Answer

- **Audio**: 30–90 seconds
- **Skills**: Listening
- **Questions**: 1–2 per exam
- **Marks**: Low — skip-worthy

**Strategy**: Skim question before listening. When audio ends, select one and move on.

---

## 6. Select Missing Word

- **Audio**: 20–70 seconds (last word replaced by beep)
- **Skills**: Listening
- **Questions**: 1–2 per exam
- **Marks**: ~0 — skip-worthy

**Strategy**: Select one and move on. No marks confirmed. Spending time here costs you time on Write from Dictation.

---

## 7. Highlight Incorrect Words

- **Audio**: 15–50 seconds
- **Skills**: Listening and Reading
- **Questions**: 2–3 per exam
- **Marks**: ~12–15 marks listening + reading
- **Negative marking**: Yes

**Strategy**: Quickly skim the transcript before audio starts. Follow the text with your cursor as audio plays. Click words that differ from what is spoken. Caution: negative marking applies — only click a word if you are sure it differs. Missing a word (not clicking it) does NOT trigger negative marking; clicking a correct word DOES.

---

## 8. Write from Dictation

- **Audio**: 3–5 second sentence
- **Skills**: Listening and Writing
- **Questions**: 3–4 per exam
- **Marks (Academic)**: ~35–40+ marks writing, ~17–20 marks listening
- **Marks (Core)**: ~35–40+ marks writing + listening

**This is the highest-scoring module in the entire exam.**

See [[write-from-dictation]] for the full three-step method.

---

## Priority Order for Listening

1. **Write from Dictation** — 35–40+ marks. Do not rush here, maximize accuracy.
2. **Highlight Incorrect Words** — 12–15 marks, moderate difficulty.
3. **Summarize Spoken Text** — 10–15 marks, requires note-taking.
4. **Fill in the Blanks** — 8–10 marks, spelling critical.
5. **All others** — click one, move on.
