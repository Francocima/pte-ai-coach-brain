## How to Write Summarize Written Text (Step-by-Step)

Follow the steps below to write an accurate and high-scoring PTE Summarize Written Text response.

### 1\. Skim the passage (10–15 seconds)

Begin by skimming the passage quickly to understand the general topic and context. Do not try to read every word at this stage. The goal is to grasp the subject and tone of the text.

### 2\. Identify the main idea

Locate the main idea, which is usually presented in the first one or two sentences. This is the central message or argument that the author is trying to convey.

### 3\. Identify 2–3 key supporting points

Scan the passage to find two to three important supporting ideas that explain or reinforce the main idea. Avoid focusing on minor examples or unnecessary details.

### 4\. Select 6–7 important keywords

Pick essential keywords and phrases from the passage. These should reflect key concepts, academic terms, or subject-specific vocabulary. Using relevant keywords improves content recognition and relevance scoring.

### 5\. Combine the main idea and key points into one sentence

Create one clear, meaningful sentence that integrates the main idea and supporting points. Ensure your sentence follows logical structure and maintains the original meaning of the passage.

### 6\. Use appropriate conjunctions

Connect the ideas smoothly using conjunctions such as:  
and, while, as, because, although, moreover, however, therefore  
These help create a complex or compound sentence, which improves grammar and structure scores.

### 7\. Maintain the one-sentence rule

Your response must be written as a single sentence with only one full stop. Additional periods will break the sentence and lead to score deductions.

### 8\. Stay within the word limit

Ensure your summary is within the required word count:

- PTE Academic: 5 to 75 words
- PTE Core: 25 to 50 words

Exceeding or falling short of the word limit will result in reduced marks.

### 9\. Proofread your response

Quickly review your sentence to check for grammar mistakes, spelling errors, punctuation issues, and sentence clarity. The sentence should read smoothly and accurately reflect the passage.

## PTE Summarize Written Text Word Limit

The PTE Summarize Written Text word limit is strictly between 5 to 75 words. You must write your response in one single sentence, which makes this task more about precision than length.

To score well, focus on:

- Covering all key ideas from the passage
- Using proper grammar and punctuation
- Avoiding unnecessary details or repetition

Even though you have up to 75 words, the ideal response usually falls between 35–50 words, where you can balance clarity and completeness without overcomplicating the sentence.

## Word Limit for Summarize Spoken Text in PTE

The word limit for Summarize Spoken Text in PTE is 50 to 70 words. Unlike the written summary task, you can write multiple sentences, but your response must stay within this range.

Key tips to follow:

- Aim for 55–65 words for a safe and balanced response
- Focus on the main points from the lecture rather than minor details
- Maintain good structure with clear sentence flow
- Avoid going under 50 or exceeding 70 words, as it may affect your score

This task tests your listening and note-taking ability, so concise and accurate summaries are essential.

## Scoring – PTE Summarize Written Text

**Content**  
Responses in the PTE Summarize Written Text task will be graded by analysing whether all of the passage’s essential points have been addressed without changing the passage’s aim or topic. If what you provide as a response represents the topic incorrectly, you will lose all points for the summary. The best summary will be able to highlight the most important part of the passage and also include any supporting details of the text, while staying within the required summarize written text word limit.

**Form**  
You will receive no points if your response in the summarize written text task is under the minimum of 5 words or beyond the maximum of 75 words. Following the official summarize written text word limit is mandatory for scoring.

**Grammar**  
Grammar is graded by analysing if the sentence’s basic structure is correct. A major clause as well as a subordinate clause are generally seen in the best responses in PTE Summarize Written Text, provided they remain within the specified summarize written text word limit.

**Vocabulary**  
Vocabulary is scored if it is considered to be relevant and appropriate to the passage available in the summarize written text task, and it must be used effectively within the given summarize written text word limit.

## FAQs – PTE Summarize Written Text

### 1.What is the PTE Summarize Written Text task?

The Summarize Written Text task requires you to read a passage and write a single sentence that captures the main idea and key supporting points. The summary must stay within the required word limit and accurately reflect the author’s message without adding personal opinions.

### 2.How many words should a PTE summarize written text response have?

For PTE Academic, the summary must be between 5 and 75 words, while PTE Core requires 25 to 50 words. Staying within the word limit is essential for scoring.

### 3.Are there any templates for PTE Summarize Written Text?

Yes, using a summarize written text template or PTE summarize written text template helps you structure your summary correctly and ensures all key points are included.

### 4.Can I use examples to practice PTE Summarize Written Text?

Yes, reviewing high-scoring summarize written text examples helps you understand effective sentence structures, complex/compound sentences, and relevant vocabulary usage.

### 5.What are the common mistakes in the Summarize Written Text task?

Common mistakes include exceeding the summarize written text PTE word limit, missing key points, using simple sentences instead of complex or compound structures, and making grammatical errors.

### 6.Can I write more than one sentence in Summarize Written Text?

No. You must write only one sentence. Using more than one full stop or creating multiple sentences will result in losing points for form.

### 7.How do I choose the main idea for the PTE summary?

Identify the central theme by reading the first one or two sentences, then confirm it by noticing repeated concepts or key phrases throughout the passage. Focus on the author’s overall argument or conclusion.

### 8\. Should I use examples in the PTE summary?

No. You should not include examples or detailed supporting information. Instead, highlight the core idea and two to three important supporting points without unnecessary details.

### 9.How can I improve my score in Summarize Written Text?

Practice identifying the main idea quickly, develop the ability to form complex sentences with conjunctions, and review high-scoring examples. Also ensure correct spelling, grammar, and punctuation.

### 10.How much time do I get for Summarize Written Text?

You are given 10 minutes per question in the Summarize Written Text task. Use this time to read, plan, write, and quickly proofread your response.
